Speakers

Writers, editors and translators who will share how stories are made, from the first note to the printed page.

  • Ines Varela Roig

    Novelist

    Writes quiet novels about loud families. Her third book is set in a seaside café.

  • Tomás Ebrard

    Short story writer

    Has published three collections of very short stories, none longer than a bus ride.

  • Noor Haddad

    Literary translator

    Translates poetry from Arabic and French, and believes every translation is a rewrite.

  • Martina Coll

    Editor

    Has spent 15 years reading manuscripts at an independent publisher.

  • Daniel Okafor

    Poet and performer

    Turns poems into live performances with music and projected text.

  • Lucia Brenner

    Children's author

    Writes picture books with her daughter, who is the toughest critic she knows.

  • Pau Serrat

    Crime writer

    Former journalist who now solves imaginary cases in a very real Barcelona.

  • Ada Lindqvist

    Illustrator and zine maker

    Draws, writes and staples her own books, one photocopy at a time.
