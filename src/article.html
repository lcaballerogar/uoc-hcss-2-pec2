Annotation opens the call for the first Alvera Short Story Prize

By Clara Ferrer, Annotation press team

12 January 2027

  • News
  • Prize
  • Short stories
  • Call for entries

Alvera City Council and the Annotation volunteer collective have opened the call for the first Alvera Short Story Prize. The competition is open to anyone aged 16 or over, and stories can be written in English or Spanish. Entries close on 28 February 2027, and the winners will be announced at the festival's closing ceremony on 25 April.

How to enter

  1. Write your story. Send one unpublished story of up to 3,000 words, on any theme. It must not have won a prize or been accepted elsewhere.
  2. Remove your name. The jury reads every story anonymously, so your name must not appear in the file. Use a pen name in the title page instead.
  3. Send it online. Fill in the entry form on the festival website and upload your story as a PDF. You will receive a confirmation email within 48 hours.

Five readers, one decision

A jury of five will read every story: editor Martina Coll, novelist Ines Varela Roig, translator Noor Haddad, a librarian from Alvera Public Library and a reader chosen by lottery among the library's members. The last seat is the jury's favourite tradition already, even before the first edition.

The first prize is €1,500, and two runner-up stories will receive €500 each. The three winning stories and seven finalists will be published in the Annotation Anthology 2027, which will be presented on the last day of the festival.

“A prize is a good excuse to finish a story,” says Clara Ferrer, one of the festival's founders. “Many people start writing and never send anything. We hope the deadline gives them the push they need.”

What the jury will value

  • An original voice and a clear point of view
  • A story that feels complete, even if it is short
  • Careful language: every word in its place

For the first time, the festival will also offer two free online sessions for participants. On 25 January and 8 February 2027, Tomás Ebrard will answer questions about short story structure and editing a final draft.

Further info

Full rules and the entry form are available on the festival website. For questions, write to prize@annotation-alvera.com.

About the author

Clara Ferrer is one of the founders of Annotation and leads its press team. Before the festival, she spent ten years organising readings at Alvera Public Library.
